On behalf of the community, I am happy to announce the availability of Spring Modulith 2.2 M1, 2.1.1, 2.0.8, and 1.4.13. The bug fix releases primarily ship the usual dependency upgrades. The first milestone of 2.2 moves to Spring Boot 4.2 M1 ( GH-1799 ) and Spring Framework 7.1 M1 ( GH-1798 ), alongside a couple of minor bug fixes and improvements around the Namastack Outbox integration and auto-configuration registration that have been ported back if applicable.
