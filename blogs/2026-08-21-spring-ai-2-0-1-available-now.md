Release notes | Upgrade notes | Reference documentation | Javadoc 2.0.1 is the first maintenance release on top of the 2.0.0 GA foundations . It is a large one: more than 80 issues and pull requests, most of them driven by feedback from teams who moved their applications onto 2.0 in the past weeks.
